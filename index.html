04Studio

Small by design. Senior by default.

Kuro is Japanese for black — the colour that makes everything around it stand out. That's the job: sharp work that puts your product in front, then gets out of the way.

We're three designers and engineers working across London and Lisbon. No account managers, no hand-offs, no junior bait-and-switch — the people you meet on the first call are the people who design, build and ship your project.

Ways to work with us

  • Fixed-scope projectMost popular A clear brief, a fixed quote and a launch date. Best for new sites, brands and products. 4–16 weeks
  • Monthly retainer A reserved slice of the studio each month for updates, experiments and new features. Ongoing · cancel anytime
  • Design sprint One focused week to shape an idea into a clickable prototype you can test or pitch. 1–2 weeks
  1. 01 — Discover

    Sharpen the brief

    Workshops, audits and a clear definition of success — agreed before anything gets drawn.

  2. 02 — Design

    Decide on real screens

    Identity and interface explored in the browser early, so feedback lands on the real thing.

  3. 03 — Build

    Ship every week

    Production-grade engineering with weekly releases you can click — not status reports.

  4. 04 — Grow

    Launch, then improve

    We measure what matters after launch and keep tuning performance and conversion.
